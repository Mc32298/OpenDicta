Prioritize onboarding and “it always works” flows: permissions, model install, shortcut setup, mic diagnostics, and clear recovery when something fails.

My recommendation

Start with A, then borrow C’s setup and recovery discipline. B should wait until the core utility is excellent, because extra surfaces will magnify the current “buttons must work” problem.
